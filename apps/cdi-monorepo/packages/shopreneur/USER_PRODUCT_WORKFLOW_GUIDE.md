# User Product Workflow Guide

This guide provides concise instructions for users to manage products in the Shopreneur application.

## Adding Products
1. Ensure you satisfy the 2-unit rule:
   - Add 1 personal product.
   - Add 1 inventory product.
2. Navigate to the 'Add Product' section in your dashboard.
3. Fill out the necessary fields for each product.

## Attaching a Review Video
- When adding a product, you can attach a review video to boost user engagement.
- Upload the video file in the specified section under the product details.

## Manage Wishlist vs Inventory
- Your wishlist allows you to save products you're interested in.
- Inventory products are those you have available for purchase.
- Ensure you regularly update your inventory to reflect current stock.

## Publishing to Marketplace
- Before publishing, review all product details for accuracy.
- Click on 'Publish' when prompted to make your product visible in the marketplace.
- Make sure to follow up with monitoring product performance after it goes live.

For further details, please refer to our full documentation.
